## The two hard truths the whole exercise converged on (don't lose these)
1. **Leading-vs-coincident ceiling.** With ~2yr filing depth we can prove at best a ~12-month-lead "end-of-cycle
   monitor," NOT a 24-month leading indicator. This is the same wall that PARKED the governance signal as a trade —
   cold strangers rediscovered it independently. The kill test is aimed exactly at this.
2. **Researcher-is-the-leak.** The flag list itself is hindsight-selected (famous blow-ups + our pilot), so even a
   positive *retrospective* result won't fully convince — only the **forward FREEZE** (§7) answers it.
