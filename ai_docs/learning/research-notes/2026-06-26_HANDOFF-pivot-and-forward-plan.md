### Carry-forward caveats (the new failure modes — different from the old one)
- **Focus beats possibility.** The old trap was too narrow (one dead idea ×18). The NEW trap is too broad ("an edge
  in anything"). Pick ONE door. This is the multi-front diffusion the strategy-reset already warned against.
- **Keep the rigor in the new domain.** Seasonal/structural patterns overfit *more* easily than equity backtests —
  same out-of-sample + multiple-testing honesty applies, or you'll fool yourself the other way.
- **Be honest about what the data sees.** Strong: corporate events, regulatory/policy, governance/promoter behaviour,
  sector narratives, the *narrative* layer of the real economy. Weak: clean physical-commodity / real-estate price
  series (pair with a free domain dataset to actually test a real-economy edge).
- **Don't let fresh-eyes become permission-seeking.** The cold read's sharpest line was "you asked a stranger for
  permission to quit." Don't repeat the pattern asking permission to *pursue*. Use fresh-eyes to SHARPEN, not to decide.
